Benefit terms to check

  • Eligibility and whether the member must stay.
  • Whether the companion shares the rate or breakfast.
  • Blackout dates and inventory limits.
  • Whether tax and service charges are included.
  • Cancellation, modification, and no-show terms.

Hotel details for photography and water activities

  • Parking should allow safe loading of cameras and wet gear.
  • Ask about laundry, balcony rules, and drying equipment.
  • Do not assume a “sea-view hotel” room actually faces the sea.
  • A resort night is valuable only if check-in time leaves enough time to use the property.
