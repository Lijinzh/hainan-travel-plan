What official forecasting can confirm

Long-range outlooks can describe seasonal rain, tropical systems, and broad regional tendencies, but they cannot confirm a specific beach, ferry, or surf window two weeks ahead. Close-range radar, warnings, marine forecasts, ferry notices, and airport operations matter more.

Mid-August conditions

Expect high heat, humidity, strong UV, fast-changing convection, and short intense showers. A “rain day” does not necessarily mean continuous rain, but thunderstorms, gusts, flooding, and marine wind can still cancel outdoor activities.

Historical ERA5 daily data for 2006–2025 suggests August rain-day rates of roughly 69.4% in Haikou, 76.0% in Wanning, and 76.9% in Sanya when a rain day means at least 1 mm. This is climate context, not a trip forecast.

Summer-flex itinerary

D1: arrival only

Fly to Haikou, collect the car, and check in. Add Qilou Old Street only if the weather and arrival time are comfortable.

D2: reduce stops

Choose Wenchang or Boao, not both. Remove the stop if radar or road conditions are poor.

Wuzhizhou, surfing, and freediving

  • Wuzhizhou: reserve D5 with D6 as the only backup.
  • Surfing: let the school choose the beginner window based on waves, tide, and wind.
  • Freediving: cancel automatically when Wuzhizhou moves to D6 or marine conditions are unsuitable.
