Reviews and Evidence Index

How to read this page

This page separates official rules, dated price samples, platform signals, and informal experience reports. A rating or old screenshot is evidence of what was visible at one time, not proof of current quality, price, or availability.

Flight prices

Historical Alipay samples for old August dates showed several Changsha–Haikou and Sanya–Changsha fares in the ¥420–508 range before estimated compulsory charges. The current seven-day route uses different dates and must be searched again in the user's actual booking channel.

Record exact date, route, flight, taxes, baggage, payment discount, and change conditions. Do not quote a bare fare as the final trip price.

Wuzhizhou Island

Repeated public themes include attractive water colour and a strong resort atmosphere, but also crowds, queueing, weather-dependent activities, and package confusion. Use official operating notices for ferries and products, then sample recent detailed reviews for current friction points.

Freediving and diving

AIDA official standards and active instructor status are hard constraints. Social posts or shop claims can suggest questions to ask, but cannot replace course structure, safety supervision, insurance, medical requirements, or certification verification.

Surfing

Recent travel reports help identify crowding, photography add-ons, and teaching-quality variation at Houhai and Riyue Bay. The actual beginner window must still be chosen from the day's waves, tide, wind, and instructor assessment.

Wanning tourism motorcycles

Rental posts can identify common models and scenic-road demand, but a shop's statement does not define legal access. Registration, licence, insurance, contract, traffic signs, and current police guidance are stronger evidence.

Evidence grades

Grade Use in this plan
A: government / association / official rules Transport law, AIDA requirements, climate, and safety constraints
B: hotel / attraction official page Product existence, published prices, and rules—not automatic trust in marketing claims
C: major OTA / media Review scale, location, and business information for cross-checking
D: social platforms / videos / travel notes Specific pitfalls and experience details; never fake precise negative-review percentages

Do a fresh recent-review sample before booking

  1. Filter for the latest one to three months where possible.
  2. Read a mixture of high, mid, and low ratings.
  3. Record recurring concrete issues rather than emotional summaries.
  4. Separate fixed problems—location, road access, room size—from changeable service incidents.
  5. Recheck official cancellation and operating rules immediately before payment.
