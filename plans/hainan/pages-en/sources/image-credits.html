Why images were not copied from travel communities or commercial libraries

Images on Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Tuchong, Visual China, travel platforms, and ordinary websites are normally protected by their creators or libraries. This project uses assets with explicit reuse permission instead of treating online images as freely republishable.
