Detailed Seven-Day Packing List

Luggage capacity

For two travellers carrying helmets and cameras, use one medium checked suitcase plus one carry-on camera bag each where possible. Keep lithium batteries, power banks, cameras, drives, medicine, documents, and essential clothing in cabin baggage.

Do not fill every container at departure; wet gear and consumables need space during the trip.

Sunscreen specification and quantity

  • Broad spectrum SPF50+ / PA++++, water resistant, suitable for face and body.
  • About 300–400 mL total for two travellers over seven days when using clothing as the first layer of protection.
  • Reapply after swimming, heavy sweat, towel drying, and according to the label.
  • Add UPF50+ long sleeves, hats, and UV400 sunglasses rather than relying only on lotion.

Clothing per person

  • Four quick-dry tops.
  • Two pairs of quick-dry shorts.
  • Two swim shorts or swimsuits.
  • One light pair of trousers.
  • One clean linen or portrait shirt.
  • One UPF long-sleeve layer.
  • One light wind/rain shell.
  • Underwear and socks appropriate for daily changes plus one reserve.

Choose colours that can combine across several shoots instead of packing separate outfits for every location.

Shoes and riding equipment

  • Drainage or water shoes.
  • Sandals for hotel and beach use.
  • Closed-heel shoes for driving and motorcycle riding.
  • Compliant helmet with clear visor, anti-fog care, light gloves, and knee protection or suitable riding trousers.
  • Do not rely on a rental shop's decorative helmet for real protection.

Water and sun equipment

  • 5–10 L dry bag and phone waterproof pouches.
  • Quick-dry towels.
  • Sunglass retainers and hat straps.
  • Electrolyte tablets or oral rehydration salts.
  • Reusable water bottles.
  • Anti-chafe care if needed.

Photography equipment

  • Camera bodies, 24–70 main lens, optional wide and telephoto lenses.
  • CPL and ND with cases.
  • At least two batteries per body, chargers, cables, and labelled cards.
  • Rain covers, blower, cleaning pen, cloths, and sealed bags.
  • Two backup drives / SSDs and a card reader.
  • Small tripod only if it will actually be used safely.

Toiletries and daily life

  • Tooth care, cleanser, moisturiser, and personal hygiene items.
  • Laundry bags and a few clips for wet gear where permitted.
  • Small tissues, wet wipes, and hand sanitiser.
  • Earplugs and sleep mask if useful.
  • Compact umbrella; never use it as lightning protection.

Medicine and documents

  • Identity documents and valid driving licences.
  • Booking screenshots and any benefit credentials.
  • Motion-sickness medicine, allergy medicine, oral rehydration salts, plasters, antiseptic, and personal prescriptions.
  • Travel insurance that explicitly covers the chosen surfing or freediving activity.
  • Emergency contacts and offline map areas.

Final check before departure

  1. Flight and baggage rules.
  2. Car booking, one-way fee, deposit, and licence requirements.
  3. Weather, flight, road, ferry, surf, and marine notices.
  4. Hotel dates, cancellation, parking, and late arrival.
  5. Battery placement and prohibited-item rules.
  6. Two independent copies of important booking and emergency information.
This remains an editable planning document.

Flights, weather, marine conditions, hotel prices, and transport rules can change. Recheck the plan before booking.
