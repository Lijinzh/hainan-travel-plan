## Car versus organised tour

A car is the most practical base for two travellers carrying helmets, cameras, and wet gear. Choose a compact SUV or roomy sedan with adequate collision and third-party protection. Confirm deposit, excess, kilometre limit, fuel policy, one-way return charge, and the exact return inspection process.

Never drive onto sand, reefs, unpaved access tracks, or flooded roads simply for a photograph.

## Local motorcycle rental

### One day only

Keep the car parked in Wanning and use a motorcycle only for a confirmed legal local section. Do not use it for airport or cross-city transport.

### Avoidance checklist

- Correct plate, registration, and valid insurance.
- Written contract naming the actual rider and damage terms.
- Helmets and safe tyres, brakes, lights, and mirrors.
- No expressways, no city prohibition zones, no unverified scenic roads.
- Do not accept “everyone rides here” as proof that a road is legal.

## Seven-day pre-departure recheck

1. Exact flight totals and baggage.
2. Written Haikou pickup / Sanya return total.
3. Current local motorcycle restrictions.
4. Weather, flood, and road notices.
5. Rental documents and insurance coverage.
