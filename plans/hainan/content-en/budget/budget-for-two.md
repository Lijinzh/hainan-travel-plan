## Costs most likely to escape control

- Close-in flight changes and baggage.
- One-way car fee, excess, and damage disputes.
- Water-activity packages and on-site upsells.
- Resort-room upgrades that do not improve the actual route.
- Weather-driven cancellation without refundable terms.

## Payment rhythm

1. Flights and cancellable car booking after the exact comparison.
2. Refundable stays next.
3. Weather-sensitive activities close to departure.
4. Keep contingency unspent until the trip.
