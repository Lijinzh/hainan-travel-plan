# Surfing Research

## Riyue Bay or Houhai

| Place | Character | Best for | Risk / drawback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Houhai village | Sand bottom, shallow white water, dense services | Complete beginners and relaxed lifestyle images | Crowds, variable teaching quality, busy water |
| Riyue Bay, Wanning | Mature surf culture and more consistent waves | A serious first lesson and action photography | Some breaks are stronger; beginners must not enter advanced areas alone |
| Shimei Bay / Shenzhou Peninsula | Better linked with coast and riding photography | Motorcycle portraits, sunset, and optional experience | Not automatically better for beginners every day |

Use Riyue Bay for the main formal lesson. Treat Houhai as a relaxed revisit or backup rather than stacking two schools into the route.

## Choosing a lesson

- At least about two hours with land instruction, warm-up, safety, and water coaching.
- Small group or clear coach-to-student ratio.
- Written price including board, leash, rash guard, insurance, photography, and shower if applicable.
- The school—not the traveller—chooses the beginner window based on tide, wind, and waves.
- Independent board rental only after the instructor confirms adequate control.

## Timing

- Do not insist on sunset if beginner conditions are better earlier.
- Use later light for photography only after the lesson and when water safety allows.
- Cancel or reschedule when thunderstorms, strong onshore wind, or unsafe shore break appears.

## Photography roles

- One person follows the lesson; the other shoots from a safe, permitted position.
- Use a fast shutter around 1/1000 s for action and slower panning only from a stable position.
- Do not stand in the impact zone, block other surfers, or enter deeper water with unprotected equipment.

## Safety floor

- Never surf alone as a beginner.
- Wear the leash correctly and protect the head during falls.
- Do not enter crowded or advanced peaks without instructor approval.
- Stop immediately for lightning, injury, exhaustion, or unsafe visibility.
