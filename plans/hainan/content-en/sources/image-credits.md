## Share-alike notes

The Yalong Bay and Haikou files retain creator, licence, and work-page attribution. Local copies were cropped, resized, and lightly adjusted for web delivery and remain shared under the corresponding licence: [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) or [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

## Why images were not copied from travel communities or commercial libraries

Images on Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Tuchong, Visual China, travel platforms, and ordinary websites are normally protected by their creators or libraries. This project uses assets with explicit reuse permission instead of treating online images as freely republishable.
