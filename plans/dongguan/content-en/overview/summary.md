## Why Xichong

- It sits naturally before the Songshan Lake transfer;
- Dapeng officially lists surfing at Xichong, and Beach No. 4 hosted a national longboard event;
- A cancelled lesson can become Dapeng / Shenzhen photography without breaking the route;
- Shuangyue Bay is better saved for a separate two-day coastal trip.
