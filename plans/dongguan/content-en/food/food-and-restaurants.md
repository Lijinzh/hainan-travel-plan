# Dongguan food and named restaurants

> Restaurant hours, branches, queues and reservations are dynamic. These are candidates researched on 12 August 2026; recheck them on Dianping / Amap and by phone. A solo traveller should not order a banquet merely to tick boxes.

## Food worth prioritising

- Roast goose with lai fun rice noodles in Houjie;
- Lychee-wood roast goose in Dalingshan, a protected local tradition;
- Daojiao pork-ball congee with crisp fried rice noodles;
- Daojiao wrapped glutinous-rice dumpling with mung bean, salted egg and pork;
- Maogen congee and Guancheng / Wanjiang water-town Cantonese food;
- Dim sum, roast meats, water-chestnut desserts and tofu pudding;
- Seasonal cured meats, lychee products and water-town ingredients.

## Eating around training

Before water sessions, choose familiar low-fat food and avoid a large meal. Afterwards, rehydrate and eat carbohydrate, protein, vegetables and soup. Avoid alcohol. Use the Guancheng day for a proper restaurant, dessert and a small night snack rather than stacking several heavy meals.

## Reservation levels

- **Contact in advance:** Course, hotel restaurants, weekend groups / private rooms, a whole goose or seasonal dish, and surf lessons.
- **Recommended:** Dongguan Old Restaurant, Heqing Shangge, Song Shan Tea House and Friday / Saturday dinners.
- **Usually walk-in:** Lai fun, congee, desserts and retail rice dumplings, though sell-outs happen.

Check unit pricing and preparation fees for seafood, portion size for whole fish / goose, and the full branch name and district before travelling.
