03

FREEDIVE FIRST

Learn to descend safely,
not to chase 46 metres

0M5M12M AIDA246M POOL
A beginner succeeds through comfortable equalisation, stable technique, rescue knowledge and knowing when to end a dive.
  • Never practise breath-hold alone.
  • Turn up when gentle equalisation fails.
  • Take more training instead of forcing a pass.
  • Rest, hydrate and avoid alcohol or a long ride after depth work.
