Mission 02: Dongguan, 16–22 August

Updated 13 August 2026. This is a solo route from Changsha. The approximately ¥3,200 course lead comes from a Douyin offer seen by the user; flexible availability has not been confirmed in writing.

Recommendation

Depart on 16 August and use this sequence:

Xichong arrival on the 16th → beginner surf lesson on the morning of the 17th → Songshan Lake that evening → freediving course on 18–20 August → Dongguan exploration on the 21st → return on the 22nd.

The current forecast still shows possible showers or thunderstorms on 16–17 August, but rain becomes more persistent from the 18th. Using the earlier window for the coast and the wetter days for indoor DiveHub training gives the main objective more protection.

Why Xichong

  • It sits naturally before the Songshan Lake transfer;
  • Dapeng officially lists surfing at Xichong, and Beach No. 4 hosted a national longboard event;
  • A cancelled lesson can become Dapeng / Shenzhen photography without breaking the route;
  • Shuangyue Bay is better saved for a separate two-day coastal trip.
