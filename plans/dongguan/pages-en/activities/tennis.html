Tennis in Dongguan: a light 25 August match only for the Beihai branch

Updated 21 August 2026. The default Shaoguan plan uses 25 August for Danxia Mountain and does not schedule tennis. Only if the Beihai branch is active, the 24th course is complete and recovery is normal should you keep a light 09:30–11:00 on 25 August match near Songshan Lake, with the court fee split equally.

Venue priority

  1. Orange C Tennis, Songshan Lake: nearby self-service lead; only confirm entry, surface, price, racket rental and 25 August morning availability if the Beihai branch is active;
  2. Shanhe Sports Center / Point International Tennis Academy Songshan Lake: indoor lead for rain and a short local match;
  3. Dongguan Tennis Center: clearer public-court rules but farther from Songshan Lake; keep it as a Beihai-branch backup.

GloBox is an optional match-making lead. Suitable players, courts and live availability must be checked in the current app or venue channel.

Match-making message

If the Beihai branch is active, I will be near Songshan Lake in Dongguan on the morning of 25 August. My level is around NTRP ____. I can serve, receive and play a scored match. I am looking for a same-level or half-level-higher player, singles or doubles, for 1–1.5 hours. Court fee split equally; I can exchange a short baseline-rally video to confirm level. Indoor court preferred.

Do not write only “intermediate / advanced”. Add:

  • NTRP level;
  • Whether you can complete a scored set;
  • Singles or doubles preference;
  • Serving, receiving and scoring ability;
  • Whether a player half a level higher is acceptable.

Freediving boundary

  • Do not pre-book formal tennis during 22–24 August;
  • The default 25th task is Danxia Mountain; play only if the Beihai branch is active, the 24th course, assessment or make-up work is complete and there is no ear pressure, headache or unusual fatigue;
  • If the course runs late, cancel tennis rather than compressing recovery;
  • Do not treat “24-hour self-service” as proof that every time is suitable for exercise.
